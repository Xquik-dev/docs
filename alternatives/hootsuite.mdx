## Source-backed Hootsuite scope

Hootsuite's official plans page lists Standard with up to 10 social accounts and unlimited scheduling. It includes posting-time suggestions, AI images and captions, Canva and Adobe Express templates, one inbox, and DM automations. It also includes 7-day mention search, sentiment analysis, 5 competitor benchmarks, and DM assignments.

Hootsuite's Advanced plan adds unlimited social accounts, custom analytics reports, saved replies, automated responses, and bulk scheduling for up to 350 posts. It also adds message routing, tagging, 20 competitor benchmarks, report delivery, 30-day mention search, and outbound post tags. The plans page lists PDF, PPT, CSV, XLSX, scheduled email, and calendar exports.

Hootsuite's publishing pages describe list and calendar views, custom approvals, task assignments, posting-time suggestions, AI posts and images, and Whiteboard planning. Its inbox tools cover comments, DMs, saved replies, automated replies, routing, agent collisions, and customer surveys. Social listening covers trends, topics, mentions, hashtags, sentiment, and websites. Enterprise options include SSO, advanced analytics and inboxes, employee advocacy, review management, compliance, chatbots, and Salesforce.

Hootsuite's plans page lists Standard at USD 99 per user/month with 10 social accounts. Advanced costs USD 249 per user/month and lists no social-account cap. Both prices use annual billing. Enterprise uses contact pricing and adds custom access, support, advocacy, listening, analytics, inbox, Salesforce, and services. The page also lists a 30-day trial and a skip-trial discount.

## Migration path

Do not move the entire social stack first. Keep Hootsuite for cross-channel planning, inboxes, approvals, analytics, ads, and listening if that is already the operating model. Move one X-only task when the output must become API data, files, signed webhooks, or MCP tool results.

Keep the test small: one task, one output, one cost model, and one downstream owner. Use Xquik for the X work that needs tweet search, follower export, post actions, media uploads, DMs, monitor tweets, webhook delivery, SDK calls, or MCP.

Price the real workload. On Xquik, Starter is USD 20/month with 140,000 credits. Top-ups are USD 0.00015/credit, webhook and stored-event management are free, and active monitors bill only while enabled.
